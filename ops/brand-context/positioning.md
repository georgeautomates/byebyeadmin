# Positioning

## One-liner

Done-for-you AI automation for UK haulage admin. Built specifically for fleet operators, not adapted from a generic AI agency playbook.

## What we do

ByeByeAdmin builds and runs AI-powered workflows that eliminate the repetitive admin that slows down haulage operations. Invoice matching, driver compliance checks, job sheet creation, subcontractor comms, daily report generation — automated and running without the operator lifting a finger.

## What we are NOT

- Not a SaaS tool (no app, no dashboard to learn)
- Not a generic AI consultancy that works across all industries
- Not a chatbot
- Not "AI strategy" or advisory — we build the actual thing and hand it over running
- Not a technology company — we're an operations service

## Key differentiators

1. **Haulage-specific.** George knows the operations, the paperwork, the regulations (DVSA, O-licence, tachograph, FORS). Most AI agencies know none of this.
2. **Done-for-you.** The client doesn't touch n8n, doesn't write prompts, doesn't configure anything. They describe the problem. We build the automation. It runs.
3. **Real results, not pilots.** Not a 3-month discovery project. We identify a workflow that wastes time, automate it, and the client sees the hours back within weeks.
4. **Small team, direct access.** Clients speak to George, not an account manager. No agency overhead.

Competitors are either:

We sit in a gap: a small, specialist operator who builds things that actually run.
