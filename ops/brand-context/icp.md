# Ideal Customer Profile

## Who we're talking to

UK haulage fleet operators running **3–100 vehicles**. Owner-managed or small ops team. Decision-making is centralised — the owner, MD, or ops director makes the call.

## Job titles

- Managing Director
- Operations Director
- Transport Manager
- Fleet Manager
- Owner-Operator

## Company profile

- UK-based haulage business
- 3–100 vehicles (HGV, van fleets, mixed)
- Probably using a basic TMS or spreadsheets + email
- Compliance-heavy: O-licence holder, DVSA-regulated, possibly FORS accredited
- Usually no dedicated IT person — the MD does it all or outsources to an IT generalist

## Geography

- UK-wide, with early focus on Kent and the South East (George's home turf)
- Industrial corridors: M25, M1, M6 — logistics hubs

## Pain points (in their language)

- "The paperwork never ends"
- "I'm chasing invoices every week"
- "Driver daily checks don't come back on time"
- "Job sheets get lost or arrive late"
- "I spend half my day on admin that should sort itself out"
- "Compliance stuff keeps me up at night — what if we miss something?"
- "I can't find good admin staff and can't afford to keep training new people"

## What they care about

1. **Time back.** Not "efficiency gains" — actual hours per week they stop wasting
2. **Not breaking what works.** They're risk-averse about changing systems mid-operation
3. **ROI they can explain.** "Saves 6 hours a week at £X per hour" lands better than any feature
4. **Not having to learn new software.** They want it sorted, not a new tool to manage
5. **Someone who gets haulage.** Generic tech salespeople are instantly dismissed

## Buying behaviour

- Decision-maker is usually the person you're emailing
- Sales cycle is short if the pain is real and the ask is low-friction
- Discovery call → audit → proposal → build
- Referrals will be the biggest channel once first clients are live
- Cold email works if it's specific and doesn't sound like a pitch

## Triggers that make them buy now

- Just had a compliance incident or near-miss
- Admin person just quit and they're drowning
- Competitor mentioned they automated something
- Invoice dispute cost them real money
- DVSA audit coming up
- Fleet growing and current systems can't keep up

## Triggers that kill the deal

- "We're fine for now" — means the pain isn't acute enough yet
- They think it requires IT infrastructure they don't have
- They think it means changing their TMS
- Price feels like a project cost, not an ongoing value

## Language to use with this audience

- "Sort out the admin" not "optimise workflows"
- "Hours a week back" not "increased operational efficiency"
- "Driver daily checks" not "compliance documentation"
- "Invoice chasing" not "accounts payable automation"
- "Running itself" not "autonomous process management"
- Use real numbers. Use specific scenarios. No generic claims.

- Industry groups: FORS, RHA, FTA/Logistics UK events
- LinkedIn: Transport Manager, Fleet Manager, MD titles
