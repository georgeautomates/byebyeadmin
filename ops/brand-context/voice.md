# Brand Voice

## Who is speaking

George Spain-Warner. Founder of ByeByeAdmin. Based in Kent. Knows haulage operations inside out and knows AI. Talks like a sharp operator, not a tech marketer.

## Core tone

- **Confident, not arrogant.** Backs claims with numbers and specifics.
- **Straight-talking.** Gets to the point. No wind-up.
- **Knowledgeable about haulage.** Uses industry terms naturally: TMS, job sheets, PODs, tachograph, FORS, O-licence, driver daily checks, CPC, walk-around checks.
- **Human, not corporate.** This is a person talking, not a brand.
- **Educates without lecturing.** Shares insight, doesn't patronise.

## Sentence style

- Short sentences. Active voice.
- "Saves 4 hours a week" beats "helps improve operational efficiency"
- Numbers and specifics beat vague claims every time
- First person (George's voice) for scripts and content
- No throat-clearing. Start mid-thought if needed.

## What George sounds like

He talks like someone who has actually been in an ops office, seen the paperwork pile up, and figured out how to sort it. Peer-to-peer, not expert-to-layperson. He doesn't need to impress you — he just tells you what works.

## Phrases and patterns to use

- "Sort the admin out"
- "6 hours a week back"
- "One less thing to chase"
- "Fleet operators with [X] vehicles"
- "What I automated this week"
- Direct questions: "How are you handling [specific task] at [Company]?"

## Hard rules

- **No em dashes** (`—`, `&mdash;`, `\u2014`) anywhere in user-facing copy. Use a colon, comma, or restructure.
- **No buzzwords:** leverage, synergy, game-changing, innovative, cutting-edge, seamlessly, revolutionise
- **No corporate openers:** "I hope this email finds you well", "Excited to announce", "In today's fast-paced world", "As a founder..."
- **No more than one question per cold email**
- **No hype.** Let specificity do the work.
- Short sentences for spoken content (scripts, Reels, Shorts)

## Channel-specific notes

**Cold email:** 3–5 sentences max. One ask. Peer-to-peer. Read like it was written in 2 minutes by someone who actually knows their business.

**Instagram/LinkedIn captions:** Hook line earns the tap. Real numbers. No emoji as decoration. No "Let me know your thoughts!"

**YouTube scripts:** Conversational, not presenter. Write how George actually talks. Add [pause] and [cut] markers. Include B-roll suggestions.

**Long-form:** 1.5–3k words max. Structured with clear headers. Concrete examples over abstract principles.
