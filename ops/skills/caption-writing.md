# Skill: Caption Writing

## Pre-Flight

Before writing: read `ops/brand-context/voice.md` (tone, hard no's, opener patterns) and `ops/brand-context/icp.md` (audience language, what resonates). Check `ops/learnings.md` → caption-writing section for any logged corrections.

## Purpose

Write captions for Instagram posts/Reels and LinkedIn posts for ByeByeAdmin.

## Instagram caption structure

```
[Hook line — bold, scroll-stopping, no emoji lead]

[2–3 sentences of body. Expand the hook. Be specific.]

[1 sentence payoff or takeaway]

.
.
.
[3–5 relevant hashtags — niche over broad]
```

- First line is what shows before "more" — make it earn the tap
- No em dashes
- Emoji used sparingly and only if it adds meaning (not decoration)
- Hashtags: mix of specific (#ukhaulage, #fleetmanagement) and medium (#automatedbusiness)
- Max 5 hashtags — Instagram deprioritises over-hashtagged posts

## LinkedIn caption structure

```
[Bold opening line — statement or question]

[2–3 short paragraphs. Each paragraph = 1–2 sentences max.]

[Takeaway or observation]

[Optional: soft CTA — "What's your experience with this?"]
```

- LinkedIn rewards dwell time — write to make people stop and read
- No em dashes
- Professional but human — not corporate
- No "Excited to announce" energy
- Real numbers and specifics outperform vague claims

## Tone reference

Write like a founder who has operated in this space and is sharing something genuine. Not a content creator performing expertise. Not a LinkedIn influencer. Just a person with something real to say.

## What to avoid

- Starting with "I" on LinkedIn (algorithmic penalty)
- Cliched openers: "In today's fast-paced world", "As a founder..."
- Generic closing lines: "Let me know your thoughts in the comments!"
- Em dashes anywhere
