# Skill: Email Writing

## Pre-Flight

Before writing: read `ops/brand-context/voice.md` (tone rules, hard no's) and `ops/brand-context/icp.md` (who you're writing to and their language). Check `ops/learnings.md` → email-writing section for any logged corrections.

## Purpose

Write cold emails, follow-up sequences, and reply templates for ByeByeAdmin outreach. All copy should feel like it came from a real person who knows haulage — not a marketing department.

## Principles

- **Short.** 3–5 sentences max for cold email. No paragraphs.
- **Specific.** Reference their fleet size, region, or a real industry detail. Never generic.
- **One ask.** One call to action per email. Usually: reply, book a call, or watch a short video.
- **No em dashes.** Use colons, commas, or restructure.
- **No buzzwords.** No "leverage", "synergy", "game-changing", "innovative".
- **No hype.** Let the specificity do the work.

## Cold email structure

```
Subject: [specific hook — question or observation]

Hi [First name],

[One sentence: why I'm reaching out, tied to something real about their situation]

[One sentence: what we do, framed as a result not a feature]

[One sentence: social proof or specific outcome, e.g. "Saved one Kent operator 6 hours a week on invoice admin"]

[CTA — single, low-friction ask]

George
```

## Subject line formulas that work

- Question: "How are you handling [specific admin task] at [Company]?"
- Observation: "[Region] fleet operators and driver daily checks"
- Result-first: "6 hours/week back for a 40-truck Kent operator"
- Direct: "Quick question about your paperwork"

## Follow-up sequence rules

- Day 3: Short bump. One sentence. "Did this land, [First]?"
- Day 7: Different angle. New hook. Not "just following up."
- Day 14: Break-up email. Honest close. Leaves door open.

## Spintax for Instantly

Use `{option1|option2|option3}` syntax for variation. Every sentence that could be varied should have 2–3 alternatives. Reference `instantly-campaigns/sequences.js` for live examples.

## What NOT to write

- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "I wanted to reach out because..."
- "Are you the right person to speak to about..."
- Anything with an em dash
- More than one question in a single email
