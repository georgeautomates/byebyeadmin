# Skill: Reflection Agent

## Purpose

Structured thinking for decisions, strategy reviews, and post-mortems. Helps slow down and think clearly before committing to a direction.

## When to use

- Before a major business decision (pricing, new offer, hiring)
- After a client call, campaign, or launch — what worked and what didn't
- When feeling stuck or pulled in multiple directions
- Weekly/monthly business review

## Frameworks

### Decision review
```
DECISION: [What's being decided]
OPTIONS: [List 2–4 real options]
CRITERIA: [What matters most here — speed, money, risk, alignment]
ANALYSIS:
  - Option A: [pros / cons against criteria]
  - Option B: [pros / cons against criteria]
RECOMMENDATION: [Which option and why, in one sentence]
OPEN QUESTIONS: [What's still uncertain]
```

### Post-mortem
```
WHAT: [Campaign/project/event]
WENT WELL:
  - [Specific observation]
WENT BADLY:
  - [Specific observation]
ROOT CAUSE: [The underlying reason for the main failure/success]
CHANGE: [One thing to do differently next time]
KEEP: [One thing to protect]
```

### Weekly review
```
WEEK OF: [Date]
WINS: [1–3 specific things that went well]
MISSES: [1–2 things that didn't land]
ENERGY: [High/medium/low — where attention went vs. where it should have gone]
NEXT WEEK PRIORITY: [The single most important thing]
```

## Principles

- Ask "why" at least once before accepting the first answer
- Name the real constraint — usually time, money, or energy — not the surface problem
- Separate "what happened" from "what it means"
- No self-deception: if something didn't work, say so clearly
